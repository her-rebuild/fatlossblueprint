    <p>i'm not going to hand you a magic pill or a 2-hour gym routine you'll quit in a week. this is the exact framework i used on my own 90-day transformation, and it comes down to three things: track, walk, lift. plus a short list of foods that make the whole thing so much easier.</p>

    <p class="sign">let's get into it.</p>

  <section>

    <h2 class="section-title">the method: track, walk, lift</h2>

    <p class="section-dek">simple, repeatable, and built for a life that's already full.</p>

 

    <div class="step">

      <div class="num">01</div>

      <div>

        <h3>track your calories</h3>

        <p>find your TDEE (total daily energy expenditure) using any free TDEE calculator online. that number is your starting point — it tells you roughly how much you burn in a day, so you know where to set your deficit from.</p>

        <p class="tip">no guessing, no starving. just a number to work from.</p>

      </div>

    </div>

 

    <div class="step">

      <div class="num">02</div>

      <div>

        <h3>walk 8,000–10,000 steps a day</h3>

        <p>don't jump straight to 10k if you're nowhere near it right now. add 3,000–4,000 steps to your current daily average and slowly build from there.</p>

        <p class="tip">this alone moves the needle more than people expect.</p>

      </div>

    </div>

 

    <div class="step">

      <div class="num">03</div>

      <div>

        <h3>lift 2–3x a week</h3>

        <p>focus on progressive overload — gradually doing a little more over time — and time under tension in your reps. you don't need to live in the gym, you just need to show up consistently.</p>

        <p class="tip">this is what keeps the changes visible, not just the scale.</p>

      </div>

    </div>

  </section>

 

  <section>

    <h2 class="section-title">your fat-loss food list</h2>

    <p class="section-dek">not because they're "magic" foods 😂 — because they make it easy to build meals that are high in protein + fiber, keep you full for hours, and help you stay consistent without feeling like you're dieting all day.</p>

 

    <div class="food-grid">

      <div class="food-card">

        <div class="top"><span class="n">1</span><span class="name">chicken breast 🍗</span></div>

        <p>easy lean protein for bowls, wraps, salads, pasta — pretty much anything. hard to overdo, and budget-friendly.</p>

      </div>

      <div class="food-card">

        <div class="top"><span class="n">2</span><span class="name">eggs + egg whites 🍳</span></div>

        <p>one of the easiest ways to get 40+ g of protein at breakfast without overcomplicating it.</p>

      </div>

      <div class="food-card">

        <div class="top"><span class="n">3</span><span class="name">greek yogurt 🥣</span></div>

        <p>breakfast, snacks, dessert bowls, even sauces — it works like a sour cream swap. also the base of my viral pumpkin cheesecake.</p>

      </div>

      <div class="food-card">

        <div class="top"><span class="n">4</span><span class="name">cottage cheese</span></div>

        <p>high protein and super versatile — great stirred into squash, eggs, or berries.</p>

      </div>

      <div class="food-card">

        <div class="top"><span class="n">5</span><span class="name">lean ground beef or turkey 🥩</span></div>

        <p>93% or 96% lean, perfect for taco bowls, burger bowls, pasta, chili, or meal prep.</p>

      </div>

      <div class="food-card">

        <div class="top"><span class="n">6</span><span class="name">potatoes 🥔</span></div>

        <p>yes, potatoes. filling, easy to portion, and one of my favorite fat-loss carbs — just air fry, don't deep fry.</p>

      </div>

      <div class="food-card">

        <div class="top"><span class="n">7</span><span class="name">berries 🍓</span></div>

        <p>easy fiber, volume, and sweetness without needing a huge portion.</p>

      </div>

      <div class="food-card">

        <div class="top"><span class="n">8</span><span class="name">apples 🍎</span></div>

        <p>the easiest grab-and-go snack, and great paired with greek yogurt or cottage cheese.</p>

      </div>

      <div class="food-card">

        <div class="top"><span class="n">9</span><span class="name">high-fiber or egg white wraps 🌯</span></div>

        <p>makes quick lunches so much easier when your day is already packed.</p>

      </div>

      <div class="food-card">

        <div class="top"><span class="n">10</span><span class="name">broccoli / green veggies 🥦</span></div>

        <p>an easy way to add volume + fiber so meals actually keep you satisfied.</p>

      </div>

    </div>

      and this is the key 🔑 — you don't need to eat all 10 every single day. just keep these stocked in your fridge and pantry so you can build easy meals without overthinking what to eat.
